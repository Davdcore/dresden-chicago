## Character

Mara is not the stronger player on paper. She wins space by returning safely, making Tobias move, and refusing to be drawn into his rhythm. When she feels pressure, she narrows her attention to the next shot rather than the score.

Her flaw is that she can underestimate herself, especially when facing someone with a better record. The club match matters because she discovers that patience is not passivity: she can set the pace and make a stronger opponent play her game, even as shifting patches of sunlight cross the court.

## Linked Event

**[The club match](../wiki.md#the-tennis-und-sauna-club-schboinktown-match)** is Mara's decisive upset over Tobias, ending 6-1, 6-0. She wins through composure and tactical focus. The shifting wind and fortunate net-cord points may have been Lobbster's influence, but Mara does not claim divine credit for her victory.
