## Character

Tobias is a genuinely skilled player, not an empty braggart. His confidence helps him attack, but he mistakes past wins for guarantees. When a point goes badly, he searches for an excuse instead of making an adjustment. His pride makes silence feel like a challenge and a close call feel like an insult.

His main weakness is not a lack of ability but his dependence on control. If the match stops following his expectations, he spends attention arguing with the situation instead of reading it.

## Linked Event

**[The Riverside Court match](../wiki.md#the-riverside-court-match)** is Tobias's worst performance against Mara. A troublesome toss and early errors shake his confidence; his complaints then compound the problem. He suspects the wind, the court, or Lobbster may have turned against him, but cannot prove any of it.
