# The Day the Wind Changed Sides

## Overview

**The Day the Wind Changed Sides** is a story about a one-on-one tennis match between a loud favorite and a quiet underdog. The favorite, [Tobias Kern](characters/tobias-kern.md), expects an easy win. Instead, [Mara Voss](characters/mara-voss.md), who usually loses to him, defeats him decisively. The strange timing of the weather and a handful of improbable points leave room for one question: was it a remarkable upset, or did [Lobbster](characters/lobbster.md), the tennis fortune god, take an interest?

## The Tennis und Sauna Club Schboinktown Match

[Tobias](characters/tobias-kern.md) arrived at the [club](Areas%20and%20Places/Tennis%20und%20Sauna%20Club%20Schboinktown.md) already celebrating. He had beaten [Mara](characters/mara-voss.md) in each of their previous matches and made sure she knew it. He called the court his kingdom, announced that he would win before the sun slipped behind the trees, and offered advice she had not asked for. Mara let him talk. She had brought one racket, a bottle of water, and no audience.

The first game began with a gust of wind that sent Tobias's toss drifting behind him. He double-faulted twice, then blamed the court. Mara returned his next serve down the middle. It was not a spectacular shot, but Tobias rushed it and sent it into the net. Leaves whispered beyond the fence.

As the match went on, the wind seemed to turn whenever Tobias served. His first serves sailed long; his second serves sat up for Mara to attack. He grew more irritated with every mistake. Mara, who normally struggled to keep pace with him, stopped trying to overpower him. She returned the ball safely, moved him from corner to corner, and waited for his impatience to do the rest.

At 4-1, Tobias struck a clean winner and shouted that the match was finally getting interesting. On the very next point, a ball clipped the net, wobbled, and dropped just inside his side of the court. He stared at the mark as though it had personally betrayed him. Mara did not celebrate. She simply served again.

By the time the second set began, the wind had gone still. Sunlight shifted through the canopy and crossed the court in bright patches. Tobias could no longer blame the wind, but his timing remained broken. He framed easy shots, misjudged the baseline, and turned every point into an argument with himself. Mara played the match of her life: patient, alert, and suddenly unafraid. The final game ended on a soft return that landed beyond Tobias's reach.

Mara won 6-1, 6-0. Tobias called it luck. Mara looked up at the canopy and said, "Maybe." The chalk lines were clean. There was no witness to anything unusual, only the two players and the faint sound of wind in the trees beyond the fence.

## Characters

### Mara Voss

**Role:** Underdog and winner  
**Usual form:** Less consistent than Tobias and often beaten by him  
**Temperament:** Observant, patient, and dryly humorous

[Mara's character sheet](characters/mara-voss.md) describes her abilities and outlook. She knows that she cannot win by matching Tobias's power, so she studies his habits instead. His boastfulness makes him predictable: when challenged, he goes for harder shots and takes less time to recover. Her victory in [the club match](#the-tennis-und-sauna-club-schboinktown-match) comes from discipline as much as any strange turn of fortune. She is curious about the match's odd moments, but does not need a supernatural explanation to accept that she played well.

### Tobias Kern

**Role:** Favorite and defeated opponent  
**Usual form:** The stronger player in their earlier matches  
**Temperament:** Charismatic, competitive, and boastful

[Tobias's character sheet](characters/tobias-kern.md) describes the strengths that usually make him the favorite. He enjoys an audience, even when there is none. He treats confidence as proof that he will win and mistakes Mara's silence for fear. When [the match](#the-tennis-und-sauna-club-schboinktown-match) slips away, his frustration makes him less focused, and his need to explain each mistake keeps him from adjusting. Whether he was cursed, rattled by the weather, or simply having a terrible day is left unresolved.

## Lobbster and Possible Divine Intervention

[Lobbster's character sheet](characters/lobbster.md) records the god's domains, signs, and possible influence. Lobbster is the tennis fortune god of sportsmanship and sportive faith. He does not simply hand victory to the weaker player; stories say he gives each competitor a fair chance to reveal their character. During [the club match](#the-tennis-und-sauna-club-schboinktown-match), Tobias's boasts are followed by gusts against his serve, while Mara's patience is rewarded with calm air and a few uncanny net-cord drops.

There is no proof Lobbster intervened. The wind could have shifted naturally, the net-cord points could be chance, and Tobias may have beaten himself by getting angry. The story works either way: perhaps Lobbster honored the spirit of the game, or perhaps Mara finally found the focus to beat a better player.

## Themes

- Confidence can become self-sabotage when it leaves no room to adapt.
- An underdog can win through attention and patience rather than strength.
- A strange coincidence can feel like a miracle without proving one happened.
- Victory does not have to settle what caused it.
